# Changelog

All notable changes to Angstloch are listed here, newest first. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the version numbers follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## Unreleased

### Added

Initial release. The README documents everything below in full.

- **Generators** (each a preset made of public steps): `generateMaze`, `generateRoomDungeon`, `generateDigDungeon`, `generateCave`, `generateNoiseCave`, `generateMine` (four layouts: fishbone, veins, pillars and rings) and `generateForest` (with roads, streams, clearings, paths and footbridges).
- **Composable steps**: `emptyMap`, `step` and `pipeline` build a map from named steps, with ordering checks, retries and a `reserved` mask. The steps are `placeScatteredRooms`, `placePartitionedRooms`, `placeClusteredRooms`, `placeMazeRooms`, `placeMineRooms`, `placeClearings`, `placeTemplates`, `digCorridors`, `digMaze`, `digCellularCave`, `digNoiseCave`, `digFishbone`, `digVeins`, `digPillarHalls`, `digRings`, `layStreams`, `layRoads`, `layClearingPaths`, `layFootbridges`, `openExits`, `openMazeRooms` and `plantTrees`.
- **Room kinds**: every `Room` has a `kind` (`RoomKind`), `'room'` for the rooms of every room placement step, `'cell'` for the cells of a cell block, `'guardRoom'` for its guard room, `'pillarHall'` for a hall of `digPillarHalls` and `'cavern'` for a cavern of `digCellularCave` and `digNoiseCave`. Halls and caverns are listed in `rooms` with shapes of their floor that block nothing, in `'pillarHalls'` and `'cave'` groups (`PillarHallsGroup`, `CaveGroup`), and the steps still join them as features; `generateMine` and the cave presets report them in `rooms`, `roomShapes` and `roomGroups`. `digCorridors`, `digVeins` and `placeMineRooms` record where they enter a hall or a cavern as an `Exit` of it, `digVeins` also where it enters any other room (it now adds `rooms` and `roomExits`), and `generateCave` and `generateNoiseCave` report `roomExits`. `createRoomMap` gives the id of the room each floor tile belongs to.
- **Cell blocks**: `placeCellBlocks`, a step that places the cell blocks of a prison, rows of identical cells along a corridor, entered at one end. Its cells are rooms that point to their block in the new `roomGroups` extra with `Room.group` (`RoomGroup`, `CellBlockGroup`). `digCorridors` joins a block through its entrance: a wall reserved `Openable` outside every room is a potential exit of a feature, which gets a door by `doorChance` and is opened through `openReserved`. With `guardRoom` (`GuardRoomOptions`), a block gets a guard room beside a tunnel that leads from the end of its corridor to the entrance, and its group a `tunnel`, or (`placement: 'through'`) stands against the end of the block, which is entered through it; `'any'` draws either for each guard room. `digCorridors` joins rooms that already have exits with the floor their doors open onto, as one piece, and records a wall it opens in such a room as an `Exit` of it. With `placement: 'lattice'`, a block, its guard room and its tunnel sit on the lattice of a lattice dungeon. `digCorridors` and `digVeins` open an `Openable` wall that has the floor of two different pieces on opposite sides, such as a block entrance that a later hall reaches, with no corridor or vein.
- **Sewers**: `digSewers`, a step that digs a sewer: a straight trunk canal of `ShallowWater` with branches at right angles, junction and head chambers, walkways of `Floor` along the water and `Bridge` tiles that keep every walkway reachable without wading. Its chambers and tunnels are rooms of the new kinds `'sewerChamber'` and `'sewerTunnel'`, features that `digCorridors` and `digVeins` join on their walkways, in a `'sewer'` group (`SewerGroup`) with its canals (`SewerCanal`) and the mouths where they end (`SewerMouth`). The walls beside the water and the rock behind each mouth are reserved `Solid`. Up to two ends of the trunk reach the border as outfalls (`outfalls`, `SewerOutfall`), the water stopping at the border wall or, with `pierceBorder`, running off the map, and the border tile beside the walkway reserved `Openable`. `openExits` takes `at: 'outfalls'` to open them as ways out. With `cisterns`, some junction and head chambers are cisterns instead, rooms of the new kind `'cistern'`: a basin of `ShallowWater` with `Floor` ledges along some of its sides (`cisternSize`, `cisternLedges`) and maybe columns standing in the water (`columnChance`, `columnSpacing`).
- **Catacombs**: `digCatacombs`, a step that digs one section of a catacomb in its area: a lattice of straight galleries of `Floor` crossing at right angles, a few of them wide main galleries (`mainGalleries`, `mainWidth`) and the rest side galleries (`sideWidth`), with the rock between them drawn from `spacing`, loops by `loopChance` and dead ends by `deadEndChance`. Each straight segment of gallery draws a character by the weights of `segments` (`CatacombSegmentWeights`): its walls lined with burial niches, short dead ends of `Floor` (`nicheDepth`, `nicheSpacing`, `nicheSides`), lined with burial chambers, small rooms behind a `Door` that face each other in pairs (`chamberSize`, `chamberSpacing`, `chamberSides`), or bare, a segment with no room for a chamber getting niches instead. A dead end may be closed by a chamber (`deadEndChamberChance`), and a chamber's walls may be lined with niches (`chamberNicheChance`). The galleries are spaced wider to leave the rock the niches and chambers need. Crypts, copies of room templates (`templates`), are placed in the area first; the galleries route round them and round the rooms placed before, and enter both by short passages from their exits, which may rejoin galleries a room cut apart, with doors by `doorChance`. It is a feature that `digCorridors` and `digVeins` join on its boundary, never digging through the rock between its galleries nor into a niche or a chamber, which are reserved `Solid`. Every chamber is a room of the new kind `'burialChamber'`, with its shape and its door as its exit. It adds a `'catacomb'` group (`CatacombGroup`) with its area, the straight segments of its galleries (`CatacombSegment`) with the character each became, its niches (`CatacombNiche`), its chambers (`CatacombChamber`), their sides typed `CatacombSide`, and its crypts. A crypt is a room of kind `'room'` pointing to the group, and a room placed before that the galleries enter gets its new exits. With `symmetry` and `symmetryChance`, the catacomb is mirrored or turned about the middle of its area: the galleries always, and the niches and chambers of a segment and its twins by the chance; with `templateSymmetry`, the crypts get mirror images too, entered by mirrored passages.
- **No corner gaps**: `digCorridors` and `digVeins` never leave a corridor or a vein touching the floor of a hall, a cavern, a sewer or any other feature corner to corner only, where a walker who moves diagonally could slip past the wall.
- **Room templates** for dungeons, mazes, mines, catacombs and forests, with rotation and mirroring. A room made from a template reports its `template`, `templateName` (the template's `name`), `rotation` and `mirrored`.
- **Exits** carved into the map border for mazes, caves and mines.
- **Symmetry** for caves and dungeons, and **areas** that confine a feature to part of the map.
- **Determinism**: every generator takes an injected `Random`, so a seed gives the same map.
- **Walking distances**: `measureDistances` measures the walk between places of a finished map, with the `manhattan`, `chebyshev` and `euclidean` metrics and a cost factor per tile, and `measureRoomDistances` between every two rooms of a result, halls and caverns included.
- **Data model**: `Grid` and `Tile` (`Wall`, `Floor`, `Door`, `Grass`, `Tree`, `Road`, `ShallowWater`, `DeepWater`, `Bridge`) with plain `{ x, y }` points.
- **Area windows**: `area.x` and `area.y` (`AreaWindow`) limit where a feature step puts its area along each axis: the `anchor` of the box (`'left'`, `'center'` or `'right'` across, `'top'`, `'center'` or `'bottom'` down) falls between `min` and `max`. Every step with an `area` takes them, and on the lattice a box may end one tile off the window. `assertAreaSpec` checks them, with a new `lattice` option, and the cave steps reject a window with `placement: 'beside'`.
- **Area gravity**: `area.gravity` (`AreaGravity`) slides a feature step's area from the random spot it was drawn on toward a side, a corner or the middle of the map (`toward`), by a share of the way (`strength`), stopping against whatever is in the way, so several copies pile up against each other. Every step with an `area` takes it, instead of a window; the cave steps reject it with `placement: 'beside'`.
- Zero runtime dependencies, ESM only, tree-shakable.

### Changed

- **Doors of optional exits in templates**: the `doorChance` of the step that places a template now decides the `OptionalExit` cells too, when the template is placed, and `RoomShape.doors` lists them, opened or not. `digCorridors`, `digCatacombs`, `openMazeRooms` and `placeMineRooms` keep that answer when they open one, instead of rolling their own `doorChance`, so a template placed with `doorChance: 1` gets doors in every exit. The exits of ordinary rooms still follow the step that opens them. The same seed gives a different map.
- **Areas**: the type of the `area` option is `AreaSpec` (was `AreaSize`), and its check is `assertAreaSpec` (was `assertAreaSize`), since it is to say more than a size.
