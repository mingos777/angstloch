# Rooms

## `Room`

| Field             | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `id`              | The room's index in `rooms`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `kind`            | What the room is (`RoomKind`): `'room'` for the rooms of every room placement step, templated rooms included, `'cell'` for a cell of [`placeCellBlocks`](steps/place-cell-blocks.md), `'guardRoom'` for a [guard room](steps/place-cell-blocks.md#guard-rooms) of one, `'pillarHall'` for a hall of [`digPillarHalls`](steps/dig-pillar-halls.md), `'cavern'` for a cavern of [`digCellularCave`](steps/dig-cellular-cave.md) or [`digNoiseCave`](steps/dig-noise-cave.md), `'sewerChamber'`, `'cistern'` and `'sewerTunnel'` for a chamber, a cistern and a stretch of tunnel of [`digSewers`](steps/dig-sewers.md), and `'burialChamber'` for a burial chamber of [`digCatacombs`](steps/dig-catacombs.md). |
| `group`           | The index in `roomGroups` of the group the room belongs to, such as the cell block of a cell (see [`RoomGroup`](#roomgroup)). Absent for a room of no group.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `x`, `y`          | Position of the room's top-left floor tile. The room's wall is not included.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `width`, `height` | Dimensions of the room's floor in tiles.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `exits`           | Indices into `DungeonResult#exits` (or the `roomExits` of the other results and of the steps) of the room's exits. A hall's or cavern's are the places where a corridor, a vein or a tunnel enters it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `exitCount`       | Number of exits of the room.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `doorCount`       | Number of exits of the room that have a door.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `template`        | Templated rooms only: the index of the room's template in `templates`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `templateName`    | Templated rooms only: the `name` of the room's template, so a game can tell its templates apart even when several steps each have a list of their own. Absent when the template has no name.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `rotation`        | Templated rooms only: the clockwise rotation the template was placed in.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `mirrored`        | Templated rooms only: whether the template was placed mirrored.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

For a templated room, `x`, `y`, `width` and `height` describe the template's bounding box, walls included, as the floor of such a room need not be a rectangle. Ordinary rooms have no `template`, `rotation` or `mirrored`; whether a room is templated is told by `template`, not by `kind`.

**Halls and caverns are rooms too.** A hall of `digPillarHalls` and a cavern of the cave steps are listed in `rooms`, so the game finds every place of the map in one list, but the steps treat them as [features](steps/dig-corridors.md#features), by their `kind`: they reserve nothing, their shape in `roomShapes` is their floor alone (the rest of the box `Unused`, with no potential exits), which blocks nothing, a later room placement step ignores it, [`digCorridors`](steps/dig-corridors.md) joins them on their boundary like any other piece of floor and never drops them, and [`openMazeRooms`](steps/open-maze-rooms.md) leaves them alone. A hall's `x`, `y`, `width` and `height` are the rectangle it was dug in, and a cavern's the smallest rectangle that holds its floor, so either may have wall in its corners, and the pillars of a hall are rock inside it.

**So are the parts of a sewer.** The chambers, cisterns and tunnels of [`digSewers`](steps/dig-sewers.md), kinds `'sewerChamber'`, `'cistern'` and `'sewerTunnel'`, are features in the same way: their shapes are all their tiles, water and bridges included, but the columns of a cistern, and block nothing, the joining steps join the sewer anywhere on its boundary, never drop it and record where they enter it as exits, as for a hall. They differ in one thing: the sewer reserves the walls beside its water `Solid`, so a corridor or a vein only ever reaches a walkway. Each is a rectangle with no wall inside it, but a cistern, whose columns stand in its water.

### Ways into halls and caverns

A hall or a cavern has no walls of its own to open, so the joining steps record where they reach its floor: every tile that [`digCorridors`](steps/dig-corridors.md), [`digVeins`](steps/dig-veins.md) or [`placeMineRooms`](steps/place-mine-rooms.md) digs beside the floor of a hall or a cavern is a way into it, and the tiles of one way that touch along an edge are one [`Exit`](#exit), at their middle tile, so a vein two tiles wide makes one exit. A tile that is the exit of a room too, or that touches the floor of two halls or caverns, is one exit of both. `digVeins` records its ways into every other room the same way: the `Openable` tile it opens. Floor that a hall or a cavern was dug into, or that a later step digs right up to it, is no exit, since no joining step dug it.

## `RoomGroup`

Rooms that one copy of a step built together form a group, listed in the `roomGroups` extra, and each of them points to its group with `Room.group`. Groups never move, so `group` stays right when `digCorridors` drops and renumbers rooms. A group says what it is in `kind`: `'cellBlock'`, `'cave'`, `'pillarHalls'`, `'sewer'` or `'catacomb'`.

A `'cellBlock'` group (`CellBlockGroup`) is a block of [`placeCellBlocks`](steps/place-cell-blocks.md):

| Field       | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `kind`      | `'cellBlock'`.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `footprint` | The box of the block's cells and corridor, its outer walls included. A guard room and its tunnel lie outside it.                                                                                                    |
| `corridor`  | The floor of the block corridor, which the doors of the cells open onto.                                                                                                                                            |
| `entrance`  | The wall tile through which the block is entered, at an end of the corridor, beyond the tunnel, or in the far wall of a guard room the block is entered through, a door or a gap once a joining step has opened it. |
| `tunnel`    | The floor of the tunnel from the end of the corridor past the guard room's door to the entrance. Absent without a guard room beside the block.                                                                      |

Two blocks themed differently, one for prisoners and one for monsters, say, are told apart by their groups.

A `'cave'` group (`CaveGroup`) is one cave of [`digCellularCave`](steps/dig-cellular-cave.md) or [`digNoiseCave`](steps/dig-noise-cave.md), in one area, and its rooms are its caverns. A `'pillarHalls'` group (`PillarHallsGroup`) is the halls that [`digPillarHalls`](steps/dig-pillar-halls.md) dug in one area. Both have one more field, `area`: the box the cave or the halls were dug in, in the coordinates of the map. A cave or an area that got no floor makes no group.

A `'sewer'` group (`SewerGroup`) is one sewer of [`digSewers`](steps/dig-sewers.md), and its rooms are its chambers and tunnels:

| Field      | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `kind`     | `'sewer'`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `area`     | The box the sewer was dug in, in the coordinates of the map.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `canals`   | Its canals (`SewerCanal`), the trunk first, then the branches in the order they were dug.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `mouths`   | Every end of a canal (`SewerMouth`), in the order of `canals`: the trunk has two, a branch one, at its far end. Where a branch meets its parent is no mouth, since its water runs into the parent's, or into the basin of a cistern there. A game themes the mouths as it likes: a culvert under bars, a sump grate, a crevasse. |
| `outfalls` | The ends of the trunk that reach the border of the map (`SewerOutfall`), at most two, in the order of their mouths.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

A `SewerCanal` is a straight canal:

| Field        | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `level`      | `'trunk'` for the canal the branches grow from, `'branch'` for every other.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `centerline` | The tiles of its middle line, as `Point`s, from one end to the other: a branch's from the tile beside its parent's water, or from the ledge of the cistern where it meets its parent, out to its mouth, the trunk's from its left or top end. With an even width, the line nearer the top or the left of the two middle ones. A tile may be a bridge. |
| `width`      | How many tiles wide its water is.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `parent`     | The index in `canals` of the canal a branch meets at right angles. Absent for the trunk.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

A `SewerMouth` is an end of a canal:

| Field      | Description                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `position` | The tile of water at the end of the canal's centerline, or the bridge that carries the ledge of a cistern over it where the canal runs into one.                                               |
| `wall`     | The wall tile beyond `position` that the water meets. Absent at an outfall with `pierceBorder`, where the water runs onto the border and off the map, and where the canal runs into a cistern. |
| `canal`    | The index in `canals` of the canal it ends.                                                                                                                                                    |

A `SewerOutfall` is an end of the trunk at the border of the map:

| Field      | Description                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `position` | The border tile at the end of the walkway beside the trunk's water, reserved `Openable`. It stays wall until [`openExits`](steps/open-exits.md) with `at: 'outfalls'` opens it into a way out. |
| `mouth`    | The index in `mouths` of the end of the trunk it lies beside: 0 for the left or top end, 1 for the right or bottom one.                                                                        |

The sewer has no direction of flow: which way the water runs, and so which mouth is a source and which a drain, is the game's call. An outfall is only where the trunk meets the border.

A `'catacomb'` group (`CatacombGroup`) is one catacomb of [`digCatacombs`](steps/dig-catacombs.md). Its rooms are its burial chambers, of kind `'burialChamber'`, and its crypts, of kind `'room'`; the catacomb itself is a feature, not a room, and its niches are not rooms either:

| Field      | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `kind`     | `'catacomb'`.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `area`     | The box the catacomb was dug in, in the coordinates of the map.                                                                                                                                              |
| `segments` | The straight stretches of its galleries (`CatacombSegment`), the horizontal ones first, row by row from the top and left to right, then the vertical ones, column by column from the left and top to bottom. |
| `niches`   | Its burial niches (`CatacombNiche`), off its galleries and inside its chambers, in the order of their segments.                                                                                              |
| `chambers` | Its burial chambers (`CatacombChamber`), in the order of their segments.                                                                                                                                     |
| `crypts`   | The ids in `rooms` of its crypts, the rooms made from its `templates`, in the order they were placed.                                                                                                        |

A `CatacombSegment` is a straight stretch of gallery between two places where the gallery does not just run on: a junction, where three or four stretches meet, a turn or a dead end. Those places belong to no segment.

| Field         | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `floor`       | The `Area` of its floor, without the junctions, turns or dead ends at its two ends.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `orientation` | Which way the gallery runs: `'horizontal'` or `'vertical'`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `width`       | How many tiles wide the gallery is, the `height` of a horizontal `floor`, the `width` of a vertical one.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `main`        | Whether it is a stretch of a main gallery, or else of a side gallery.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `character`   | What the segment became: `'chambers'` when its walls are lined with burial chambers, at least one, `'niches'` when they are lined with niches, at least one, which is also what a segment drawn with chambers becomes when none fits, or `'bare'` when they are neither, which is what a segment drawn with niches becomes when none fits. A chamber at its dead end does not count. |

A `CatacombNiche` is a burial niche, a short dead end of `Floor` one tile wide off the wall of a gallery or of a burial chamber:

| Field     | Description                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `tiles`   | Its tiles, as `Point`s, from its mouth, the tile next to the gallery or the chamber, to its back.                                                              |
| `side`    | The side it is on (`CatacombSide`): of its gallery, `'top'` or `'bottom'` of a horizontal gallery, `'left'` or `'right'` of a vertical one, or of its chamber. |
| `segment` | The index in `segments` of the segment it opens onto, or of the segment of its chamber.                                                                        |
| `chamber` | The index in `chambers` of the chamber it opens onto. Absent for a niche off a gallery.                                                                        |

A `CatacombChamber` is a burial chamber, a room of kind `'burialChamber'` with one door onto a gallery:

| Field     | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `room`    | Its id in `rooms`, which holds its floor, and through its exit in `roomExits`, its door.                                                                                                                                               |
| `segment` | The index in `segments` of the segment its door opens onto.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `side`    | Which way it lies from its gallery (`CatacombSide`): a side of the gallery, as for a niche, or for a chamber at a dead end, the end it closes, `'left'` or `'right'` of a horizontal gallery, `'top'` or `'bottom'` of a vertical one. |
| `deadEnd` | Whether it closes a dead end of its gallery, its door in the end wall, rather than standing beside the gallery.                                                                                                                        |

## `Exit`

| Field      | Description                                                                                                                |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `position` | The wall tile of the gap, as a `Point`.                                                                                    |
| `rooms`    | Ids of the rooms the exit belongs to: one, or two when a single door joins two rooms that face each other across one wall. |
| `door`     | Whether a door was placed.                                                                                                 |

For a hall or a cavern, `position` is the first tile dug beside its floor (see [ways into halls and caverns](#ways-into-halls-and-caverns)), and `rooms` may list two halls or caverns that a single tile joins, or a room and the hall or cavern its exit opens onto.

## `createRoomMap`

`createRoomMap(result)` tells which room each tile of a finished map belongs to: an `Int32Array` with one number per tile, row by row (`y * width + x`), the id of the room whose floor holds it, or -1. It takes anything with a `grid` and `roomShapes`, the result of a generator or of [`pipeline`](composing.md#pipeline). Every room counts, of every kind, through the `Floor` cells of its shape: an ordinary room's floor, a templated room's floor cells, a hall's floor without its pillars, a cavern's floor, every tile of a sewer's chambers and tunnels, water and bridges included, and of its cisterns but their columns. Walls, exits and doors belong to no room, since an exit may belong to two, and neither do corridors, veins, tunnels or any floor outside the rooms.

```ts
const result = pipeline(
  emptyMap({ width: 80, height: 50, tile: Tile.Wall }),
  [digPillarHalls(), placeScatteredRooms(), digCorridors()],
  { random: mulberry32(42) },
);
const roomMap = createRoomMap(result);
const id = roomMap[player.y * result.grid.width + player.x] ?? -1;
const room = result.rooms[id]; // undefined in a corridor
```
